Your wheel

The ISO diameter is the first number in a marking like 622-28 or 28-622 on your tyre sidewall.

Tyre squash

How much the tyre flattens under you. It shortens the distance travelled per turn by about this much.

      Circumference is the wheel measured round, before anyone sits on it. The figure above it is what the wheel actually travels.

      Measure it instead

      A roll-out beats any estimate — it accounts for your weight, your pressure and your tyre's real shape. Mark the tyre and floor at the valve, sit on the bike, roll forward in a straight line, and measure between the marks.
